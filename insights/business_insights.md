# Business Insights from E-commerce Data Analysis

## 1. High-Value Customers
The analysis identified the top 5 customers contributing the highest revenue:
- CUST_04705
- CUST_00061
- CUST_01984
- CUST_00525
- CUST_03721

These customers represent high-value segments and should be targeted with loyalty programs, personalised offers, and retention strategies.

---

## 2. Product Category Performance
The most popular product category based on order volume is:

- Sports

This indicates strong customer interest in sports-related products. The business can:
- Increase inventory in this category  
- Launch targeted promotions  
- Expand product variety  

---

## 3. Payment Method Insights
The payment method generating the highest revenue is:

- Credit Card

This suggests that customers prefer secure and convenient payment options. The business can:
- Offer cashback or rewards for card payments  
- Partner with banks for promotions  

---

## 4. Key Recommendations
- Focus on retaining top customers through loyalty programs  
- Invest in high-performing product categories like Sports  
- Optimise payment experience to encourage high-value transactions  

---

## Conclusion
This analysis demonstrates how SQL can be used to extract meaningful business insights from raw data, supporting data-driven decision-making in e-commerce.
